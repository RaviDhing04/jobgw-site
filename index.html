jobgw: personal job gateway

jobgw is a personal, local tool that Ravi Dhing runs on his own computer to collect job openings for himself and his family. It is not a public service, has no other users and is not offered to anyone else.

To find openings, it reads job-alert emails in the owner's own Gmail account, with read-only access.
